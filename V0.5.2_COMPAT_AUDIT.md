## 5. `engines` / `peerDependencies` ranges and resolution results

The three candidate declarations — `engines.dsh`, peer `@deepseek-ai/dsh-credentials`, peer
`@deepseek-ai/dsh-session` — are all **`>=0.2.1-rc.2 <0.2.2-0`** (`package.json:43`, `:81`, `:82`).
The old `0.5.1` declaration was the exact string `0.2.1-rc.2` in all three fields.

Measured facts: **28 matrix rows** (21 candidate-range cells + 7 old-pin rows);

[`../_phase052-compat/analysis/semver-matrix.json`](../_phase052-compat/analysis/semver-matrix.json),
[`../_phase052-compat/analysis/logs/05-semver-matrix.log`](../_phase052-compat/analysis/logs/05-semver-matrix.log).

Why the semantics agree: `includePrerelease` changes matching only for prerelease versions, and the
lower comparator `>=0.2.1-rc.2` carries a prerelease on the tested `0.2.1` tuple, so `0.2.1-rc.3`
and `0.2.1-rc.4` satisfy the range under default semantics too. `0.2.2-rc.1` is rejected for an
ordering reason, not a flag reason: numeric `0` sorts below `rc`, so `<0.2.2-0` excludes every
`0.2.2` prerelease and `0.2.2` itself. An alternative range such as `^0.2.1-rc.2` would admit
`0.2.2-rc.1` under the gate and `0.2.2` under both — too wide — so `<0.2.2-0` is the narrowest
conventional upper bound that keeps the whole `0.2.1` line
([`analysis/logs/10-alternative-ranges.log`](../_phase052-compat/analysis/logs/10-alternative-ranges.log)).

### 5.3 What the range means at its boundaries

- `0.2.1-rc.1` is rejected by the lower bound; `0.2.1-rc.2` and `0.2.1-rc.3` are the verified hosts.
- `0.2.1-rc.4` and `0.2.1` final are **admitted by construction but untested and not claimed**. The
  range cannot express "only rc.2 and rc.3" without enumerating them; `<0.2.2-0` is the narrowest
  conventional bound that keeps the whole `0.2.1` line. This is the documented support policy, and
  it is restated in `README.md`, `docs/DSH_INTEGRATION.md` and `RELEASE_NOTES_V0.5.2.md`.
- `0.2.2-rc.1` and `0.2.2` are rejected. **`0.2.2` is not supported.**

### 6.2 The `overrides: {"rolldown": "1.2.14"}` build-reproducibility pin

**Why it exists.** The released v0.5.1 `lib/client.js` was built with **rolldown 1.2.14**. A fresh
resolution of the regenerated lockfile resolved **rolldown 1.2.15**, and the two versions emit
different bundler region-marker comments (`//#region module` + `//#endregion` vs a single
`// module`), so `lib/client.js` differed by 160 comment-only bytes even though no source changed.
Pinning `1.2.14` restores byte-identical output. Measured on three preserved bundles
(`build/toolchain/`, log `toolchain/normalize-compare.log`):

| Build | `lib/client.js` bytes | SHA-256 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| released v0.5.1 (rolldown 1.2.14) | 68 877 | `9D62B1849118BF9E49CAD7E7636FDDA4CEBEC6A43EDA275A37D72C1C5EF746AC` |
| fresh lock, before override (rolldown 1.2.15) | 68 717 | `48FB668910CF93209DE0DD2EDA59D39BFC8E34E90654805F4F5DCCF783F8E7B0` |
| 0.5.2 with override (rolldown 1.2.14) | 68 877 | `9D62B184…5EF746AC` (identical to released) |

All three variants normalize to the same text (66 520 chars, SHA-256
`48FB6689…8E7B0`), which localizes the pre-pin difference to comment syntax, no code byte.

**Scope.** It is an install-time resolution rule: devDependency-resolution only, never installed by
a consumer (npm applies a dependency's `overrides` only from the root project), adds nothing to the
runtime import graph, and changes no `require`/`import` in `lib/` and no `engines`/peer field. It
exists solely so this candidate's `lib/**` bytes are reproducible against the released v0.5.1 build
(`MANIFEST_CHANGES.md` §4).
